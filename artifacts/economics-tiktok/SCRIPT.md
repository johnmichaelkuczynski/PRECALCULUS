# Economics 101 — 16:9 screen-led promotion

## Director's treatment

- **Promise:** A student can move from a four-week course plan to reading, tutor help, practice, and review in one place.
- **Identity:** The existing Economics course interface: warm ivory background, navy headings and controls, white cards, muted dividers, Georgia-style serif headings, clean sans-serif body text.
- **Format:** 16:9 widescreen, exactly 40 seconds. The course UI is composed as a desktop-width app view, not an enlarged portrait frame.
- **Hero:** The app occupies every pixel of every frame. Its persistent left navigation, course content, controls, and feedback belong to the app, not a promotional layer. No title card, device bezel, lower third, or floating marketing copy.
- **Motion:** A visible tap indicator selects actual-looking interface controls; course screens open in place; tutor text and feedback resolve inside their own app panels.
- **Arc and payoff:** Start on a dashboard overview; narrow to one lesson and a tutor exchange; widen to progress, practice, and reviewed work. The final review remains on screen until the loop cuts back to the dashboard.
- **Sound:** One friendly, authoritative, masculine British narrator. Narration describes the action visible at that moment. No music, sound effects, or ambient track. Timing below is a target until actual voice audio is measured.

### Shot 03 — 13.0–20.5 / 7.5 s — Ask the tutor

**Choreography:** 13.0–13.5 tab completes opening; 13.5 tap enters input; 13.7–15.2 question types; 15.2–15.5 tap on in-app send arrow; 15.5 question settles as bubble; 15.7–16.2 three-dot waiting state; 16.2–19.1 answer progressively reveals in readable chunks; 19.1–19.8 answer holds; 19.8–20.3 tap moves toward sidebar “Analytics”; 20.3 tap; 20.3–20.5 analytics header replaces chat. **Handoff:** The selected Analytics tab persists into Shot 04. **Assets/technique:** Chat components, deterministic text reveal, app tap indicator. **Sound:** Narration only, ending while the answer is on screen.

### Shot 05 — 26.5–33.5 / 7.0 s — Practice and feedback

## Production and verification constraints

- The desktop-width app fills the entire 16:9 export. All taps, tabs, text, and feedback are part of the visible app simulation; the preview-only scene controls must not be recorded.
- No music or sound effects. Do not substitute unrelated audio if text-to-speech cannot be authorized.
- Keep key text away from the outer frame edges; the app sidebar and content remain readable at 16:9 export size.
- Narration must be generated, audible, checked against scenes, and trimmed or paced so the 40-second export has no dead tail.
- This TikTok film is separate from the widescreen `qr-course-demo` artifact, which must remain unchanged.
